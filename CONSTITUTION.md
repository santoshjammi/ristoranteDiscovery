# Ristorante Intelligence Platform — Product Constitution v1.0

> This document is the constitutional foundation for all product, engineering, and business decisions. Every feature, every line of code, every go-to-market decision must align with this constitution. If a decision conflicts with this document, the constitution wins.

---

## 1. Vision

**Become the operating system for Indian restaurant discovery and growth in the North Carolina Triangle.**

Not a restaurant SEO tool. Not a restaurant ERP. Not a marketing automation platform. The operating system that connects diners with the right restaurant, restaurants with the right customers, and the market with the right intelligence.

---

## 2. Mission

Transform unstructured restaurant information into retrieval-ready intelligence that measurably increases discoverability, revenue, and growth for Indian restaurants in the NC Triangle.

---

## 3. Business Philosophy

### 3.1 We sell outcomes, not features

Restaurants don't buy "schema generation" or "FAQ optimization." They buy:
- "Rank for 'Best Biryani Near Lenovo'"
- "Become Google's Recommended Restaurant"
- "Get Found in AI Search"
- "Increase Lunch Traffic"
- "Dominate 'Near Me' Searches"
- "Turn Reviews into Customers"

Every feature must be traceable to a customer outcome. If a feature cannot answer "how many additional customers will this generate?", it does not ship.

### 3.2 Discovery is 10%. Intelligence is 90%.

There are hundreds of restaurant discovery products. Very few answer:
- Why is this restaurant succeeding?
- What should a new restaurant copy?
- Which cuisines are growing?
- Which locations are saturated?
- Which menu items generate the highest ratings?
- Which restaurants have declining sentiment?

Those are intelligence questions. The platform answers them.

### 3.3 The moat is data

Every menu, every review, every dish, every price, every cuisine, every restaurant, every location, every FAQ, every schema, every trend — collected, structured, and made queryable. This proprietary intelligence becomes harder to replicate with every restaurant added.

### 3.4 Build a network effect

Every restaurant belongs to a cluster: cuisine, community, location, competitor cluster, audience cluster. Improving one restaurant improves recommendations for everyone else in its cluster.

## 6. Product Pillars

### 6.1 Discovery Intelligence
Search, recommendations, conversational discovery. The entry point — but only 10% of the product's value.

### 6.2 Restaurant Intelligence
Deep profiles, reputation analysis, operational trends, competitive positioning. Every restaurant becomes an intelligence object with a "Restaurant Genome" instead of a "Restaurant Page."

**Restaurant Genome dimensions:**
- Identity (name, cuisine, price position)
- Operations (hours, service style, capacity)
- Menu (complexity, signature dishes, pricing)
- Service (quality, speed, hospitality)
- Atmosphere (ambience, noise, decor)
- Customer Base (audience segments, retention)
- Popularity (trends, footfall estimates, peak hours)
- Digital Presence (social, review velocity, GBP health)
- Growth (trends, trajectory, expansion signals)
- Reputation (sentiment, consistency, authenticity)
- Competitive Position (market share, differentiation)
- Unique Selling Points
- Weaknesses
- Predicted Success Score

### 6.3 Customer Intelligence
Review mining, sentiment analysis, preference extraction, behavioral patterns. Every review becomes structured knowledge.

Extract from reviews:
- Food quality (★)
- Service quality (★)
- Parking (★)
- Ambience (★)
- Value (★)
- Cleanliness (★)
- Staff (★)
- Noise level (★)
- Kids friendliness (★)
- Waiting time (★)

### 6.4 Market Intelligence
Geography, cuisine trends, pricing analysis, demand patterns, investment opportunities.

For any location, answer:
- Restaurant density
- Cuisine density
- Price density
- Competition score
- Growth score
- Demand score
- Walkability
- Parking availability
- Average ratings
- Average spend

### 6.5 Growth Intelligence
Competitive benchmarking, menu optimization, expansion planning, revenue opportunity detection.

### 6.6 Predictive Intelligence
Forecast popularity, identify emerging neighborhoods, detect declining restaurants, recommend new concepts.

## 7. Product Principles

### 7.1 Increase Revenue
Every feature must trace to "how many additional customers will this generate?" If it can't, it doesn't ship.

### 7.2 Improve Consumer Trust
Recommendations must be evidence-based, not paid. Transparency builds long-term trust.

### 7.3 Reduce Restaurant Effort
One-click improvements over configuration. The restaurant should not need to understand SEO, schema, or AI.

### 7.4 Produce Measurable Outcomes
Every score must be explainable. Every recommendation must be actionable. Every improvement must be measurable.

### 7.5 Build Proprietary Intelligence
The data collected across all restaurants creates intelligence that no single restaurant could generate alone. This is the moat.

## 13. Engineering Directive

### 13.1 Architecture Principles
- Every feature must improve discoverability
- No operational restaurant features (no POS, no inventory, no reservations)
- Metadata-first architecture
- Retrieval-oriented design
- Semantic consistency enforcement
- Asynchronous enrichment pipelines
- Queue-based processing
- Rebuildable enrichment jobs
- Stateless API architecture

### 13.2 Scoring Principles
- Every score must be explainable
- Every score must have evidence
- Every score must generate recommendations
- Every score must be semantically grounded
- Every score must improve discoverability
- No arbitrary percentages
- No vanity metrics
- No black-box scoring
- No keyword spam rewards

### 13.3 UI/UX Principles
- Warm, hospitality-first, not enterprise-heavy
- Recommendation over configuration
- Guidance over complexity
- Evidence over vanity
- Progressive disclosure over feature overload
- Every screen must feel simple
- Every score must be explainable
- Every recommendation must be actionable
- Avoid technical exposure
- Maintain hospitality emotion

### 13.4 Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TailwindCSS, shadcn/ui
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (primary), pgvector (future), Redis (future)
- **AI:** Local-first (Ollama), cloud fallback (Gemini/OpenAI)
- **Processing:** Playwright, OCR, PDF parser, AI enrichment pipelines
- **Deployment:** Docker, Nginx, Cloudflare
