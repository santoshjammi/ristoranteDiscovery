## 1. Category Definition

**We are not a restaurant SEO tool. We are not a restaurant marketing platform. We are not a restaurant analytics dashboard.**

We are **Restaurant Intelligence** — a new category.

| Category | What it sells | Why it's wrong for us |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant SEO | Keywords, backlinks, GBP optimization | Too narrow. SEO is one input, not the answer. |
| Restaurant Marketing | Campaigns, social media, ads | We don't execute. We tell you what to execute. |
| Restaurant Analytics | Dashboards, metrics, reports | Too passive. We recommend, prioritize, and quantify. |
| Restaurant ERP | POS, inventory, reservations | We don't operate restaurants. We make them discoverable. |
| **Restaurant Intelligence** | Answers, decisions, outcomes | We own the decision layer between data and action. |

**Our category:** Restaurant Visibility Intelligence / Restaurant Decision Intelligence.

## 2. Market Position

### What we own

The **decision layer** between restaurant data and restaurant outcomes.

```
Restaurant Data
        ↓
Restaurant Intelligence
        ↓
Prioritized Decisions
        ↓
Business Outcomes
```

### What we don't own (and won't)

- POS systems
- Reservation systems
- Inventory management
- CRM
- Campaign automation
- Social media scheduling
- Customer service chatbots

### Why this matters

Every other product in the restaurant tech stack generates **more data**. We're the only product that turns data into **decisions**.

## 3. The Five Questions (Decision Hierarchy)

```
  1.  Am I discoverable?
          ↓
  2.  How am I performing?
          ↓
  3.  Why is this happening?
          ↓
  4.  What should I do next?
          ↓
  5.  Where should I grow?
```

This is not a feature list. This is a **decision hierarchy** — each question naturally leads to the next, mirroring how restaurant owners actually think.
