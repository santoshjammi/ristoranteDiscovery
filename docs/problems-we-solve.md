# Problems We Solve — Restaurant Intelligence Platform

> *One intelligence engine. Five business questions. One decision hierarchy.*

---

## The Decision Hierarchy

These aren't five independent questions. They're a **progression** — each naturally leads to the next, mirroring how restaurant owners actually think:

```
1. Am I discoverable?
           ↓
2. How am I performing?
           ↓
3. Why is this happening?
           ↓
4. What should I do next?
           ↓
5. Where should I grow?
```

**Example:** *"I'm getting fewer customers."*

→ Am I being discovered?
→ If yes, how am I performing compared to competitors?
→ Why am I losing?
→ What should I fix first?
→ Once I'm winning, where do I expand next?

## The User Journey

| Stage | Customer Goal | Platform Capability |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | Understand online visibility | Visibility Intelligence |
| **Diagnose** | Measure digital performance | Digital Performance Intelligence |
| **Explain** | Identify root causes | Intelligence Engine |
| **Improve** | Prioritize next actions | Decision Intelligence |
| **Expand** | Find growth opportunities | Market Intelligence |

**"Improve" is the only stage that asks the customer to act.** Everything before it builds confidence that recommendations are grounded in evidence.

## The Five Problems

### 1. Restaurants Are Invisible in Modern Discovery

Not just Google Search, but Google Maps, AI assistants, local recommendations, voice search, food platforms, and future AI discovery channels.

**The problem:** Restaurants lose customers daily because they don't rank across the expanding surface area of digital discovery. They lack structured data, semantic optimization, and AI-ready content that modern search engines and agents require.

**What we sell:** Measurable discoverability across every channel that matters.

---

### 2. Restaurants Don't Know How They're Performing Digitally

Every digital signal — reviews, ratings, menu changes, social mentions, pricing shifts — contains competitive intelligence, but it's scattered across platforms and buried in noise. Restaurants can't explain why their performance changed last month, why a competitor is surging, or what moved their rating from 4.2 to 4.0.

**The problem:** Restaurants have no unified view of their digital health — discoverability, reputation, competitive position, customer sentiment, market presence, and AI search visibility.

**What we sell:** Continuous digital performance intelligence that benchmarks and trends every signal.

> *Note: We use **Digital Performance Intelligence** — not "Performance Intelligence" broadly — because the platform specializes in digital signals, not operational data (POS, revenue, table turns, labor costs). This scope can broaden as operational data is integrated.*

---

### 3. Restaurants Waste Their Most Valuable Asset: Customer Feedback

Every review, menu, image, social mention, and operational signal contains actionable intelligence — but it's unstructured, unaggregated, and unanalyzed. Restaurants don't know why ratings dropped, what customers actually think, or what to improve first. Valuable feedback is ignored noise.

**The problem:** Hidden customer intelligence is trapped in unstructured signals across dozens of platforms.

**What we sell:** Structured intelligence extracted from every signal — so restaurants know what customers are saying, feeling, and demanding.

---

### 4. Restaurants Can't Decide What to Fix First

Restaurants are overwhelmed with possible improvements — fix the menu, update photos, respond to reviews, optimize for AI search, adjust pricing. Without prioritization, nothing gets done.

**The problem:** Restaurants have too many possible actions and no evidence-based way to rank them by business impact.

**What we sell:** Prioritized, evidence-backed recommendations with quantified ROI for each improvement.

---

### 5. Restaurants Make Expansion Decisions Without Market Intelligence

Opening or expanding a restaurant is a multi-million-dollar blind bet. Owners, chains, and investors don't know which cuisines are underserved, which locations are saturated, where demand is growing, or what price point the market supports.

**The problem:** Expansion decisions are made on gut feel while competitors with data win.

**What we sell:** Data-driven market intelligence for confident expansion decisions.

## Positioning Statement

> **The Restaurant Intelligence Platform answers the five business questions every restaurant owner must answer to attract more customers, outperform competitors, and grow with confidence.**

Everything else — SEO, review mining, competitor analysis, market mapping, ROI, AI optimization — is evidence collected to answer these five questions.

---

## Architecture Note

The platform is **one intelligence engine**, not five products. It continuously collects signals from the restaurant ecosystem, transforms them into business intelligence, and answers the five questions. The technology (knowledge graph, ontology, semantic normalization, scoring engine) is what enables the platform — not what customers are buying.
