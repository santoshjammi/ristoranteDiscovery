# Video Script — Market Perception

> **Title:** Know What's Wrong. Know What to Do Next. Measure the Improvement.
> **Duration:** ~90 seconds
> **Tone:** Confident, warm, precise — not hype, not technical. Hospitality intelligence, not software.
> **Format:** Live-action + motion graphics hybrid. Restaurant environments intercut with clean data visualization.
> **Target audience:** Restaurant owners, chain operators, marketing agencies, investors.

## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- **Restaurant footage:** Warm, natural light. Real kitchens, real dining rooms, real people. No stock-photo sterility.
- **Motion graphics:** Clean, minimal, typography-forward. Dark background with warm accent colors (amber, terracotta, deep green). Data visualizations are simple — no 3D charts, no unnecessary complexity.
- **Transitions:** The five questions should feel like a journey downward — each question naturally leading to the next. Use vertical scroll or depth-of-field transitions.

### Audio
- **Narrator:** Warm, authoritative, not salesy. Think a trusted consultant, not a TV announcer.
- **Music:** Ambient, building slightly through Scene 3, peaking at Scene 4, resolving gently at the close. Think *Hans Zimmer's "The Blue Planet"* meets *Ólafur Arnalds* — not corporate elevator music.
- **Sound design:** Subtle kitchen ambience in Scene 1. Clean, quiet data sounds (soft clicks, gentle pulses) during the engine visualization.

### Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary background | Deep warm charcoal | #1A1817 |
| Text primary | Warm white | #F5F0EB |
| Accent — confidence | Amber | #D97706 |
| Accent — growth | Deep green | #059669 |
| Accent — warning | Terracotta | #B91C1C |
| Data surfaces | Warm gray | #292524 |

### Typography
- **Headlines:** DM Sans (geometric, clean, confident)
- **Body:** Inter (readable, warm at text sizes)
- **Data labels:** JetBrains Mono (precision, technical credibility)

### Aspect Ratio
- **Primary:** 16:9 (website, YouTube, sales deck)
- **Social cut:** 9:16 vertical (Instagram Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn) — same script, tighter framing on the question sequence
